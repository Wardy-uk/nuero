# Every manifestation of NEURO and SARA — where they stand, 12 Sep 2026

A code-level audit of all eight surfaces, done by reading each client's source and
diffing the routes it actually calls against `backend/server.js`. Nothing here is
taken from a HANDOFF or a commit message. `Wardy-uk/nuero-ios` was cloned fresh at
`ca87d55` (123 commits — it now HAS a remote, which was the top risk in its own
handoff and is closed).

⚠ **This supersedes `docs/handoff-ios-parity-2026-09-11.md`.** That document listed
sixteen server features iOS did not render. iOS has shipped **49 commits since**,
and has closed all but four of them. Read the list below, not that one.

## The tab registries agree

`SaraTabs.swift` and `tabs.jsx` carry the same twelve ids in the same order with
the same default (`surface`) and the same `revealsSecondary` rule. Three shells
(phone, kiosk, iOS) plus the laptop through the phone build. That part is done.

## Gaps — iOS has it, the web does not

- **Face ID / biometric gate** in front of both apps.
- **Change the PIN** without signing out (web has sign-out, no change).
- Background HealthKit sync; local notifications with a deliverability check;
  a Live Activity for the running session; on-device outbox receipts.
- **Lock-screen accessory widgets** (`accessoryInline` / `Circular` / `Rectangular`).

## The kiosk is the same app with four things closed

Not gaps — decisions, each enforced in `sara/backend/src/routes/neuroProxy.js`:

- `health` is **not a door** (body data behind an unauthenticated desk screen). The
  Today screen names it as not shown rather than failing.
- `journal`, `vault`, `vault-hygiene`, `plaud` were doors until 11 Sep and no screen
  used them. Closed.
- Nothing outbound: `/api/actions` approve/reject is refused locally.
- Mic and push are **capability**-gated, not device-gated (`speechRecognition.js`,
  `pushUnsupportedReason`), so the same code gives the laptop Electron window no mic
  and says why.

⚠ **`sara/backend` still mounts `/api/actions` ahead of the allowlist** — but the
router is now a stub whose approve/reject/list verbs were removed on 11 Sep, with
the reason written into the file. Verified, not assumed.

## The widgets are the one place Scriptable is still ahead

`sara/widget/neuro-attention.js` (1,572 lines) **fetches `/api/attention` live** with
a token in the iOS Keychain, and renders the agenda, an Open-Meteo forecast,
readiness, the weekly-target ring, a work/personal stack flip and a self-updater.
The native `SaraWidgets` **does not fetch at all** — it renders what the app last
wrote to the App Group, greys out past six hours, and adds her field plus the Live
Activity. Its header says it "finally retires Scriptable"; functionally it does not
yet. Either close the gap or stop saying that.

The Apple Watch remains blocked on Xcode 27's `arm64e` CoreDevice against an Intel
Mac — a hardware fact, not a code one.

## Operational parity, which is not a code question

- `sara/*/dist` is gitignored and built on the target. The Pi kiosk does not update
  itself; on 11 Sep it was three commits behind with a two-day-old `dist` while
  looking entirely healthy. **A parity claim about the kiosk is a claim about what
  is deployed there, not about `main`.**
- The laptop shortcut passes a URL as an argument (`SARA.vbs`), so which build the
  laptop is running is a property of one shortcut and cannot be read from the repo.

**Three of the four iOS gaps are written and pushed** (`nuero-ios` `9aafeb2`):
the estimate on a running session, friction on Today, and the held lane on Tasks

## The widget, corrected

⚠ **Two corrections to the audit above.** Readiness and the forecast ARE already
native — `SaraWidgets/SensesWidget.swift`, 11 Sep — so the Scriptable-only tiles
were the **weekly-target ring**, the **agenda**, and **live fetch**, not the four
originally listed.

The week now travels on `SharedSnapshot` and renders as NEURO's own sentence plus
a monochrome `Gauge` in `.accessoryCircular` (`b8ab095`). An unset week has no
ring — nil, never zero.

The agenda was **written and then removed**: its only source on that side is
`AttentionFeed.dashboard`, an untyped `AnyPayload` whose `list(key:)` falls back
to "the single array-valued key", which is the net that hid four wrong-key bugs in
the 10 Sep menu audit. A reader with no trustworthy writer is this repo's
signature failure. It goes in when the dashboard is typed.

⚠ **Live fetch is blocked on the CREDENTIAL, not the execution budget.** A widget
extension is a separate process and `Keychain` sets no `kSecAttrAccessGroup`, so
it cannot read the app's PIN. Closing it means a shared keychain-access-group
entitlement on both targets — and an entitlement mistake there fails at INSTALL
and takes the whole app down. That is a decision about where a credential to a
personal server may live, so the header now says what the widget is instead of
claiming to have retired Scriptable.

## The iOS apps stopped being a list of lists — 13 Sep 2026

⚠ **I had claimed parity and had not measured appearance at all.** The 12 Sep
audit compared ROUTES and JUDGEMENT — which routes each client calls, and whether

That is a wider claim than anything that was checked, and it is the correction

### The measurement

Of **41 SwiftUI view files, 5 had a visual language of their own** and 35 were
stock. The five that felt right (`AttentionSurfaceView`, `SaraPrepView`,
`FieldView`, `ReadinessView`, `WeatherView`) were exactly the five with a palette.

⚠ **And the number over-counted SARA.** `SaraShell` forces
`.preferredColorScheme(.dark)` for the whole app, so her screens were never
white — they were the STOCK GROUPED LIST in dark mode, which is Settings at
night. A smaller gap than the count implied, and a reminder that a metric
measuring "has no literal colour" is not measuring "looks wrong".

### What was actually missing: a palette, not taste

NEURO's look is **twenty custom properties in `frontend/src/index.css`** and iOS
had none of them. `backend/scripts/export-design-tokens.js` generates
`Theme.swift` into the sibling `nuero-ios` checkout — the `export-widget-to-vault`
idiom: one source, one generator, re-run on change. ⚠ The two repos cannot share
code, so the drift is made visible on the side that OWNS the truth: the generated
file carries the source hash and `design-tokens.test.js` fails when it is behind
(mutation-checked — changing `--warning` without re-exporting fails two).

⚠ **`NeuroUI` and `SaraUI` are deliberately different.** NEURO is the desk —
bordered cards, hard palette. SARA is the presence — material chrome, serif voice,
sitting on the field. Re-skinning her onto `NeuroCard` would have made the two
apps identical, which contradicts the division of labour. `Palette` is injected at
each app root so the seven NeuroKit views rendering inside BOTH ask their host
rather than picking.

⚠ **The primitives carry the honesty rules, not just the colours**: `NeuroGap` is
the only amber, `NeuroEmpty` cannot be reached without a successful read having
been claimed, and a badge's tone is a MEANING. Doing that by hand on 28 more
screens is where it would have drifted.

⚠ **Fonts are names only.** The PWA pulls IBM Plex from Google Fonts; an iOS app
needs the TTFs bundled and a `UIAppFonts` entry. `Theme.font` falls back to the
system face at the same size — a missing font must never be a blank screen.

### The dominant failure mode, now measured

**Four instances in two days of a value computed FOR honesty, carried on a
payload, and read by nothing** — no error, no empty screen, the honesty simply
absent from the surface:

| field | who computed it | who read it |
|---|---|---|
| `askedSurface` | `sara-surface.js`, 4 tests | nothing, on either iOS app |
| `knownGaps` | `wins.js` | nothing, on ANY surface — so its text rotted, naming a function deleted on 26 Aug |
| `signals.estimates` | `initiation-signals.js` | the web only |
| `quickWins` / `winsToday` | `/api/adhd` | decoded by `TodayDashboard`, rendered by neither iOS screen |

⚠ This is worth a standing check rather than another audit: **a payload field with
no reader is the same species as a table with no writer**, and this repo already
has `inert-tables.test.js` for the second.

Out of iOS, into the desktop: **ambient**. `AdhdPanel` had no mention of it, so

### The widget fetches, and the measurement is why

Nick reported the device: **woken on her own ONCE, 21 hours earlier**. So
`BGAppRefreshTask` is not running in any useful sense — while the widget's own
timeline budget is far larger. The file the app writes was the bottleneck all
along, and a home screen reading a file nothing may update shows yesterday while
looking healthy. The widget now fetches, falling back to that file on any failure,
with an ADDITIVE shared-keychain credential so no existing item moves and nobody
is signed out.

### Found on the way

- ⚠ **The iOS Vault screen had never worked.** `/api/vault` mounts
  `requireApiKey`; the PIN does not open it, so every launch answered 401 and
  rendered as a vault with nothing in it.
- ⚠ **`/api/1to1/cadence` was added** so the cadence state is composed once
  server-side rather than re-derived in Swift. Its first cut read the frontmatter
  key names back out of `foldDetected`, which answers `{lastHeld, nextDue,
  booked}` — every report came back "ok" with no date, a team that looks
  perfectly up to date.
- ⚠ **STILL THREE COPIES OF THE CADENCE RULE.** `PeopleBoard.get121Status`
  reimplements it in the browser, carrying its own fixes (Isabel Busk's stale due
  date; Sebastian Broome's stale booking hiding a 48-day gap) and its own wording.
  Replacing it with the new route is NOT a swap — the server's label is the
  tracker's phrasing, and a straight substitution would change the copy and risk
  losing that nuance. Consolidating means making the route rich enough for all
  three callers first.
